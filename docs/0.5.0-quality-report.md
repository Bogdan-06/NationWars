## Checklist 9 changes

- Release and save schema advance to 0.5.0/data version 2.
- The ten-minute passive-income calculation uses a $120 current-capital base,
  applies the doctrine multiplier to active capital/structure income, and then
  adds `$8 * max(0, member count - 1)` outside that multiplier. Existing $6
  upgrade increments remain.
- `/nation info` includes the capital chunk and the exact current ten-minute
  payout rather than an incorrectly scaled estimate.
- `/nation create` has no direct arguments and retains the existing doctrine UI
  followed by chat-name entry. `/nations` is not registered.
- `/configure` adds `Puppets`, default `true`. `/configurate` and the
  misspelled `Satelites` setting alias are not registered; correctly spelled
  `Satellites` remains.
- Debug roots remain visible to permission-level-4 operators even when their
  technical gate is off, so they can return a clear disabled error. Nation
  deletion is now `/configure deletenation <country>`; it requires permission
  level 4, is audited, is not debug-gated, accepts full names/IDs, uses normal
  cleanup, and resynchronizes OPAC.
- USA city income is 0.2x current capital income; British coast income is 0.1x.
  Soviet base capital income remains zero while upgrade and member income work.
- Italy's random building payout is $2. Civil War is renamed Kingdom of the
  South, and its +10-second recapture penalty is limited to tracked core claims.
- Italian Push-over, betrayal penalties ($250; France $750), French offensive
  maintenance (1.5x), Soviet market price (1.25x), Soviet sub-two-attacker
  defense (2x), and Romanian lost-core maintenance (+0.1 each) were already
  implemented and were preserved for regression coverage.

## Implementation surface

This is a targeted feature pass, not another architectural rewrite.

- Command/configuration changes are concentrated in `ConfigCommands`,
  `DevCommands`, `NationCommands`, `command/NationCommand`,
  `command/MarketCommand`, and the new `PuppetCommands`/`command/PuppetCommand`.
- Formula and doctrine changes are concentrated in `NationRules`,
  `service/EconomyService`, `NationEvents`, and `Doctrine`.
- Puppet rules/state use `service/PuppetRules`, `service/PuppetService`, and
  focused additions to `NationStore` rather than replacing its existing war,
  claim, or save implementations.
- Peace/trade integration is limited to `PeaceDealMenu`, `NationTradeMenu`, and
  the existing store validation/application paths.
- Save compatibility additions are limited to data version 2,
  `DataMigrationService`, `DataIntegrityService`, and new persisted puppet/war
  fields; repository/coordinator timing remains inherited.
- Player text is available in matching English, Romanian, Spanish, and Polish catalogs;
  mod metadata and Gradle release version are 0.5.0.
- Regression coverage is added to the existing test suite plus focused puppet
  rule/service/state tests. This report does not claim exhaustive in-game test
  coverage; the manual matrix remains part of release acceptance.

## Puppet system

- Owners have `/puppet`, `propose`, `accept`, `reject`, `pacify`, `agitate`,
  `liberate`, `automate`, `release`, `annex`, and `war`.
- A normal peace deal can make its receiver the proposer's puppet.
- A puppet starts at 100 points. Claiming requires at least 50, an independence
  war requires more than 150, peaceful liberation requires 200, and annexation
  requires 0 or three lost independence wars.
- Thresholds do not trigger automatic outcomes. `/puppet liberate` and
  `/puppet automate` are equivalent manual commands.
- Agitate and pacify change points by +10/-10 and each has a 600-second
  cooldown. Trade-derived point effects share a 120-second throttle: foreign
  accepted trade +1, rejected master offer +1, and a one-sided gift from master
  to puppet -1. Trades remain valid while their point effect is throttled.
- Points are clamped to 0-200 and frozen during an independence war.
- A lost independence war subtracts 50 points and increments its loss count.
  Three losses prevent another attempt and let the master annex regardless of
  points.
- Independence-war peace, surrender, and capital/last-claim outcomes restore
  all captured land and apply no ordinary land or money terms. A puppet victory
  only releases it; a puppet loss preserves the relationship.
- A master receives 20% of each puppet's generated national passive income,
  rounded to cents before recurring trade obligations are paid. Tax received
  from a lower level is not counted as newly generated taxable income.
- Annexation transfers claims, non-negative treasury, and members to the
  master, releases the annexed nation's direct puppets, removes the puppet
  nation, and resynchronizes OPAC. Personal player balances are not transferred.
- Multiple direct puppets and multi-level relations are supported, with at most
  one master per puppet and no cycles. When a country with existing puppets is
  puppeted, its current direct puppets are released; the newly puppeted country
  may acquire new direct puppets later if doing so does not create a cycle.
- `Puppets=false` suspends actions, restrictions, point effects, tax, and new
  peace terms while retaining saved relations. A release attempt is refused
  during an active independence war so it cannot orphan the war state.

## Corrective defects addressed during implementation

- The Italian recapture check previously treated any captured claim as a core;
  the 0.5.0 caller now requires the war-start core snapshot.
- The debug setting previously hid useful operator roots; registration and
  execution gating are now separate.
- Nation deletion now cleans puppet relations/proposals and releases dependents.
- A mid-independence-war `/puppet release` was found during review and is now
  rejected instead of deleting the relation beneath an active war.
- FIX 0.5 restores the intended ten-minute income cadence and makes `/nation
  info` show the exact amount paid on that cadence.
- Scorched Earth now temporarily bypasses OPAC for the full hostile action
  surface in active enemy war claims, including block, fluid, container,
  entity, and player-caused explosion interactions. Established peace-offer,
  enemy-bed, and respawn locks remain explicit Nation Wars rules.
- Nation deletion moved to the operator configuration branch and now performs
  a post-delete existence check plus OPAC/display-name synchronization.

## Conservative decisions

- The checklist phrase "delete Satelites" is interpreted as removing only the
  typo. The working `Satellites` configuration is preserved.
- `Puppets=false` does not erase relationships or migrate them away.
- A master gift affects points only when the puppet receives at least one
  positive money/claim/recurring-income asset and gives none of those assets.
- Puppet proposals have no newly invented expiry because the specification did
  not provide one; invalid references are removed during normalization.
- Independence war resolution never leaks ordinary peace, surrender,
  capitulation, land, or money outcomes.
- Third-country join requests and alliance/guarantee defense calls are rejected
  for independence wars, keeping their directional outcome unambiguous.
- No automatic annexation or liberation was added at 0/200 points.

## Compatibility and review risks

- Existing version-1 saves migrate to version 2 on load; version-0 saves first
  receive the existing identity-preserving migration. Backup/recovery and
  immediate-save timing are intended to remain unchanged.
- The passive-income amounts and doctrine amounts are deliberate Checklist 9
  balance changes; FIX 0.5 changes only their payout cadence to ten minutes.
  Claim cost, maintenance, war justification/capture values
  outside the listed Italian rule, spy probabilities/timers, market mechanics
  outside Soviet's preserved multiplier, peace costs, alliance behavior, and
  OPAC behavior are not intentionally changed.
- `/nations`, `/configurate`, direct `/nation create` arguments, and the typo
  `Satelites` are deliberate compatibility removals requested by the checklist.
- Puppet proposals are persistent and manually resolved; servers should not
  assume they expire.
