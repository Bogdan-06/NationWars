# Nation Wars 0.5.1 manual test checklist

Use a copy of a world. Test with OPAC installed and at least two players where
the step requires opposing nations.

## Commands and configuration

- Confirm `/nwdev` exists for an eligible operator and `/nationwarsdev` is unknown.
- Confirm `/nation city` is unknown.
- Run `/configure` and verify `Stealing=true`, all three global multipliers are
  `1.0`, and `MemberIncome=10`.
- Set each new numeric setting, restart, and confirm the value persists. Restore
  the defaults before gameplay comparisons.

## Economy

- Create a one-claim nation and verify its maintenance is $0.
- Add a second normal claim and verify default maintenance is $8. Germany should
  pay $10.80 at its 1.35 doctrine multiplier.
- Capture a capital and verify the former owner no longer receives the capital
  exemption while it does not own that capital.
- Change each global multiplier and verify it composes with, rather than
  replaces, the doctrine multiplier.
- Compare passive income with 10 and 11 members; the 11th member must add no
  income at the default member limit.
- Verify Britain gets $16 for each counted member after the first.
- Verify the United States receives $180 from an active, unupgraded capital at
  default settings and has no city command. Verify American mining/farming
  rewards did not also gain the passive-only 1.5x effect.

## Doctrine and war

- Capture a United States claim during an active war and verify its national
  treasury loses exactly $200.
- Capture a capital and verify the war continues with no automatic capitulation
  or additional land transfer.
- Verify Britain receives no coastal capture modifier and Italy receives no
  hill/mountain defense modifier.
- Give Britain five claims per member and verify 1.5x maintenance; reduce below
  that ratio and verify the multiplier stops.
- Put Italy into an active war and verify its maintenance is multiplied by 0.8.
- Verify no American Isolation distance scaling is applied to claim cost.

## Protection and trade

- With `ScorchedEarth=true`, physically break a block by hand/tool in an enemy
  claim during an active war. Also verify placement and explosion access.
- With `ScorchedEarth=false`, verify OPAC protection remains active.
- With `Stealing=false`, verify a player in a foreign nation cannot use paid
  protected-container/door access, while a nationless player still can.
- Try sending a nation trade offer to an active-war opponent and verify it is
  rejected. Confirm an offer between nations not at war still works.

## Compatibility and localization

- Load a copy of a 0.5.0 save and verify nations, claims, wars, puppets, market
  listings, spies, and old city/coast persistence data load without loss.
- In English, Romanian, Spanish, and Polish clients, inspect the changed
  doctrine lore and Wall Street Crash notice for unresolved translation keys.
- Restart once and verify configuration and nation data remain intact.
